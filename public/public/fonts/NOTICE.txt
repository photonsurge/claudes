Noto Color Emoji — flags-only subset (U+1F1E6–U+1F1FF, COLRv1).
Fetched from Google Fonts' own served slice of the family:
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Color+Emoji
  (the subset whose unicode-range is U+1f1e6-1f1ff)

Copyright Google Inc. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
  https://openfontlicense.org
  https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/blob/main/fonts/LICENSE

Self-hosted on purpose: the Chromium inside OBS on the encoder host ships no
font covering the regional-indicator block, so flag emoji rendered as .notdef
boxes on air while looking correct in desktop Chrome. Serving the glyphs from
our own origin makes the broadcast independent of the encoder's system fonts.
See public/src/app/globals.css.
